Agent frameworks tend to treat permissions as an afterthought: either the agent
can do everything, or a human approves every call. colmena sits in between.

Three ideas hold it together:

- **Scoped autonomy** — policy is YAML per role and per agent, compiled to
  regex and evaluated deterministically. No model decides what a model is
  allowed to do.
- **Mandatory review** — workers submit through a review gate; a central
  auditor scores the work, and a hook blocks the agent until the loop closes.
- **Earned trust** — an append-only ELO ledger per role, with time decay. Human
  overrides always win over the score.

The whole thing is filesystem-based: YAML for policy, JSONL for the audit
trail. No network calls and no database in the hot path, which is what keeps
the evaluation under 15ms and the audit trail honest.
